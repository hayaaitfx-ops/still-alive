The Creed

PRINTED IN WHITE / PAGE 02

I stopped asking the mirror
for a reflection and started
asking it for room.

[ 01 — THE HOLE ]

There is a hole where the person used to be. Not a wound. A door. Everything loud falls through it, and what is left is white, and the white is not empty — it is unclaimed.

[ 02 — THE MASK ]

A face is a rumour you keep telling. So I keep a better one in my pocket: white, cracked, smiling with too many teeth. It never blinks first.

[ 03 — THE BLADE ]

One cut is a sentence. Mine is a paragraph. It is heavier than it looks, which is the only honest thing about it.
