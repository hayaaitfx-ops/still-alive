NO. 001

No matter what you do, I will eventually become myself. Not the version that was convenient. Not the version that kept quiet. The one that was always here, waiting under the noise.

There is no dignity in pretending you don't want anything. Reach. Even when the frame cuts you off at the wrist. Even when no one is looking. Even when the light is bad.

I stopped asking the static to become clear. Some signals are only true while they are breaking. All is well. All is well. All is well. The repetition is not a mistake.

You finally have no reason to be valuable. No use, no proof, no performance. Just the blue hour and a body that keeps going. Congratulations. You were never required to be useful.
